About

RF & microwave engineering, from electromagnetic design to prototype testing.

I am an RF & Microwave Engineer specializing in passive RF components, microwave filters, and antennas for cellular communication systems. My work combines electromagnetic simulation, RF hardware development, and prototype validation.

I completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at K. N. Toosi University of Technology. My master's thesis focused on designing a two-band diplexer using the coupling matrix method. My bachelor's thesis addressed crossed-dipole antenna design and interaction with nearby base-station antennas.

Through academic and industrial projects, I have worked on antenna elements and arrays, microwave filters, cellular repeaters, RF PCBs, and antenna measurements.
