01 About & interests

Working where fields
meet practical design.

I enjoy taking an electromagnetic idea from its underlying model to a prototype that can be tested.

My background is in electrical engineering, with a focus on telecommunications, fields, and waves. I completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. at K. N. Toosi University of Technology, where my thesis work addressed base-station antennas and coupling-matrix-based diplexer design.

Alongside academic research, I have worked on antenna development, cellular repeaters, microwave components, and RF measurement. My research interests include antenna interactions, passive microwave networks, and practical electromagnetic design.
